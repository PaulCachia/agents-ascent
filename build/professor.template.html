One professor per climber

Your professor

Every climber on the Ascent gets their own professor: a Claude Project that carries the brief below, reads your camps, hours and quiz scores straight from the shared database, tests you, and re-plans the rest of the climb with you at each checkpoint. Setting one up takes about five minutes.

Set-up

Start your climb

On the Ascent page, tap Sign in, enter your email and open the magic link on the same device. Then open My profile (your picture, top right): name, start date (your week 1), a photo if you like, and the end goal you want the professor to steer towards.

Create a Claude Project

In Claude, make a new Project called something like Agent's Ascent — professor. Projects live under your own Claude account; any plan that includes Projects works. Turn on web search for the Project if it is a setting on your plan, so the professor can read the database and the course.

Paste the brief as the Project's instructions

Copy the brief below (the button copies the whole thing) into the Project's instructions. It tells the professor how to find you, how to read your progress, how to quiz you, and how checkpoints and plans work.

Link the professor to your profile

Copy the Project's web address into My professor on My profile. The Talk to my professor button on the Ascent page then opens it.

First conversation

Open the professor, give your name, and say where you are. It reads your rows, sets this week's work, and tells you how check-ins and quizzes will run. From then on you come back weekly, and at the end of each phase for a checkpoint.

Not using Claude?

The brief works with any assistant that can hold standing instructions and open a web address: a ChatGPT custom GPT with browsing on, a Gemini Gem, or another AI with a system prompt. On My profile, the i next to My professor gives one-click set-up for each: it copies the brief and opens the right page, and lists the steps for that service. An assistant that cannot open links can still be your professor; you paste your rows into the chat instead.

Open the Markdown file
The brief is a public file in the course repository, so a professor can be updated for everyone at once: edit professor/PROFESSOR.md, rebuild, and re-paste. The database key in it is the public read-only key; nothing in the brief can write.
