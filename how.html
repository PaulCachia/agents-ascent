How the Ascent works

Twenty-four camps up one mountain. At every camp you repeat the same short loop, and your professor bends the route around your goal, your time and what you already know.

The loop at every camp

  1. 1Learn

    Watch and read the camp's resources, then do its exercises.

  2. 2Practise

    Practice quizzes: as many as you like, never posted, with explanations.

  3. 3Test

    8 questions, 12 minutes. 70% passes the camp.

  4. 4Climb

    The camp is passed and your climber moves up the mountain.

  5. 5Check in

    Tell your professor what you did and what confused you.

  6. 6Re-plan

    Your professor sends a plan for what's next. One tap applies it.

Then back to step 1 at the next camp. The summit is a real launch.

Who does what

You

  • Study the camp and do the exercises
  • Press ▶︎ to put study time on the clock
  • Take the practice and the test
  • Log a check-in each week

Your professor

  • Reads your progress, hours and test results
  • Teaches what's shaky and asks you to explain it back
  • Builds your personal plan, starting at your first chat
  • Re-plans at the end of each phase

The site

  • Holds the course, the same for everyone
  • Lays your plan on top of it
  • Marks the tests and moves your climber
  • Keeps your progress in your account

Your climbing partner

  • Climbs the same mountain at their own pace
  • Shows up as a climber in their own colour
  • Tap them to see their climb

The tests

  • Practice is unlimited, never posted, and shows the right answer and why. Use it before the test.
  • The test is 8 random questions, 90 seconds each. The clock starts when the first question appears. You can go back and change answers until you submit.
  • 70% (6 of 8) passes the camp. Every test posts its score with the attempt number beside it, and a pass closes that camp's test.
  • Not passed? A retake opens 24 hours later. After two attempts without a pass, the third waits 72 hours and a session with your professor.
  • Closing the test doesn't stop its clock: it keeps running on the server.

Your plan, on the map

Everyone shares one course. Your professor's changes sit on top of it, just for you, and show on your mountain. My course lists them under Changed for me.

Getting set up

  1. Sign inOn the Ascent page, tap Sign in and open the email link on the same device.
  2. Fill in your profileYour name, start date, end goal and a photo for your climber, on My profile.
  3. Set up your professorAbout five minutes: a Claude Project with the brief. Step-by-step guide.
  4. Connect it (recommended)Lets your professor send plans straight to your Ascent page. On My profile, under Professor connector.
  5. Have your first chatPress Talk to my professor. It asks about your goal and time, then sends your first personal plan.
  6. Start climbingPress ▶︎ to start the clock and open Orientation, the first camp.
