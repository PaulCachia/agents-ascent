 The clockStudy sessions

 The guideYour professor

Every climber gets their own: a Claude Project that reads your camps, quizzes you, and re-plans the climb with you at each checkpoint. Set-up takes five minutes.

How the loop works

1 · Build the week's modulesFollow the course tab, pausing each video to make the agent do the step and explain it. Put the time on the clock.
2 · Pass the checkpointMark the camp "Checkpoint passed" in the Ascent when you can do the module's "ready to move on" test from memory.
3 · Report to the professorOpen your professor's chat (the button on the Ascent page) and say what you built and what confused you. It reads your camps, hours and check-ins straight from the database, quizzes you, and tells you the score to record on the module row.
4 · Re-plan at checkpointsAt the end of each phase you and your professor review the climb against your end goal: expand what grips you, lighten what drags, never drop the essential spine. The professor hands you a plan; you apply it on the Ascent page.
Built in week 1 of the course as the first deployed project. Hosted on GitHub Pages; every climber's progress lives in one Supabase database under Row-Level Security: anyone can view a public climb, only its owner can edit it. Rebuilding both by hand is part of Phases 4 and 5.
